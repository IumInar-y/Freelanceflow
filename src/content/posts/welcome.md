This is the second post on the new blog. The first explained **why** we set this up; this one is a short "what to expect" so the index has more than one item to render.

Posts here will be short (under 600 words), opinionated, and grounded in the work the product is doing day-to-day. We are not aiming for a high publishing cadence — **the goal is a small number of high-quality posts per year**, not a daily newsletter.

Topics we expect to cover:

- The voice fingerprint and how it shapes a rewrite.
- What the Proposal Quality Scanner scores, and how that maps to the thing clients actually triage on.
- New product surfaces, when they ship and what they are for.

If a reader is looking for a deep technical doc, that lives elsewhere — this surface stays close to product practice.
