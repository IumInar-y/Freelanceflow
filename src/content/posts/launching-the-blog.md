title: Launching the FreelanceFlow AI blog
description: Why we built a writing surface for the product, what will live here, and what it isn't.
pubDate: 2026-08-05
---

Every product team eventually has to decide where the writing lives: the docs site, the marketing site, a Medium mirror, or nothing at all. We picked a fourth option — a small, MD-driven blog that lives in the repo — and this post is the short version of why.

The honest answer is that **the proposal rewrite is the product**, and a help center isn't the right shape for that kind of writing. We need short, opinionated posts where we can correct our own past posts, ship a wrong-then-fixed example, and link straight into the rewrite flow. A blog is the smallest surface that does that.

We will not be writing about AI more broadly, and we will not be writing about every topic adjacent to freelancing. Posts here will mostly be about **the proposal rewrite itself** — what the rewrite flow does, how the voice fingerprint is built, why certain proposals get interviews and others don't.

If a post is wrong, the next one will say so and link to the corrected version. That is mostly what this surface is for.
